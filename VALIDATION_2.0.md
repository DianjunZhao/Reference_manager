## Formula and physics boundaries

The deterministic fixture exercised a small ar5iv-shaped inventory and exact anchored formula claims; it does not measure the completeness of live ar5iv conversions or establish physical correctness of model derivations. Typed steps carry stable identity/order, before/after TeX, rule or assumption, equation dependencies, evidence anchors, source-document hashes and epistemic status. The F05 chain check rejects missing endpoints/conclusions and discontinuous steps; its 32-step limit is a hard validation bound, not a truncation rule. A bounded syntax check may reject malformed input or request manual review for unsupported macros; it cannot establish algebraic or physical equivalence, and physics status cannot be self-promoted from `needs_manual_physics`. A timeout, cancellation or missing claim does not become `complete`. The separate synthetic performance fixture contains 20 inventories/5,000 formulas; this does not establish live ar5iv corpus coverage or a corpus-wide inventory hash.
